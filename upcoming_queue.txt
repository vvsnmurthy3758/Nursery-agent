High-density guava plantations using a 3x3m spacing system can yield up to 40 tonnes per hectare.
Guava trees managed with Ultra-High Density Planting (UHDP) are spaced at 1x2m.
When planting guava in saline soils, commercial growers should add 3 Kg of Gypsum per plant.
Traditional mango farming allows 40 to 70 trees per acre, but modern techniques increase this to 200–300.
